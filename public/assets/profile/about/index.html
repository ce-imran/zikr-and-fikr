Detailed Biography

Ahmad Khliquzzafar

"To serve Islam, contribute to the reformation of society, and specialize in Comparative Religion and Islamic Jurisprudence."

Ahmad Khliquzzafar is the son of Md. Muzaffar Hussain. He is a dedicated student of Islamic Studies, currently pursuing his Alimiyyah education at Al-Jamiatul Ashrafia Mubarakpur, one of the most prestigious Islamic seminaries.

With a profound interest in Comparative Religion and Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh), Ahmad is focused on understanding the complexities of modern social issues through the lens of traditional Islamic scholarship.

He has successfully completed specialized studies in Qirā’at and is actively involved in teaching, helping others connect with the correct recitation of the Holy Quran.

His mission is driven by a desire for societal reform and the preservation of the intellectual heritage of Islam. He believes that knowledge is not merely information but a light that guides character and conduct.

Our Mission

To provide authentic Islamic guidance rooted in the Quran and Sunnah, addressing the spiritual and intellectual needs of the modern Ummah.

Our Vision

To see a society reformed through the light of knowledge, where every individual embodies the beauty of Islamic character (Akhlaq).
